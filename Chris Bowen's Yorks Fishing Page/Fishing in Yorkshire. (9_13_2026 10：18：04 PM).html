DON'T

forget your fishing licence.

Before fishing any venue on this Web site you must first obtain an Environment Agency rod licence. Rod licences are available from any Post Office in England and Wales. Details of rod fishing Byelaws and angling information can be obtained from Regional Environment Agency Offices. Fishery Byelaws may vary between different Environment Agency Regions. If in doubt, please check first.

Before you
tackle up.

For your own safety, it is absolutely vital that you do not fish within 50 metres of any overhead wires if you are using a carbon rod or pole. If you see wires and are not too sure what they are for still treat tham as DANGEROUS; do not take a chance it may be your last.
