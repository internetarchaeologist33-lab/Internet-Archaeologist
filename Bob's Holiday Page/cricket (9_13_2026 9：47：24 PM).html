Tuesday July 21st, 1998

Bob's English Cricket Adventures

Last Friday Chalfont St Giles played the final of the Timberlake Cup, a 15-overs midweek knockout competition among the local clubs. We made the final after a convincing semi-final win against Holmer Green the previous Friday. Our opponents in the final at Winchmore Hill were Penn and Tylers Green, and after a steady start they piled on the runs towards the end to finish with 143-6 off their 15 overs. Chalfont St Giles never really came to terms with the required run rate, and the loss of early wickets made the task for the middle order a little too stiff. We finished with 108-7, but made our mark for the season by claiming the runners-up spot in a keenly contested event. I now have a trophy to bring home to prove that I have played cricket in England.

Rain interrupted play on Saturday against Little Kingshill and washed out our Sunday mach all together. I had the consolation of taking 3-40 on the Saturday, including a wicket with my third delivery, and I now sit at the top of the club bowling averages where I will remain until the next time I bowl.
