THE BEST & THE WORST OF IT

Best TV Shows

  • The Garry Shandling Show - sitcom
    During its four(?)-year run on HBO, this show stunningly mixed an acerbic and cynical humor with show-biz drama ... which is to say, self-indulgent bombast. There's not a likeable character in the cast, a risky nod to honesty only palely reflected in its network counterpart, Seinfeld. Still in nightly reruns on one of the HBO channels.

  • The West Wing - drama
    Still currently in production in 2000, Martin Sheen is the President, superbly portraying the gravity and scope of all that job entails. The show's best feature is its ability to rise so far beyond the typical Hollywood cynicism of anything remotely political that it has actually made me proud to be a citizen of the United States of America. Brilliant scripts focus on dedicated people working for the betterment of the nation ... I believe it can happen. So does this dramatic series.
