Terraise O'Hagan: Adele Taylor

 

Terraise was first seen in the 1992 series of Byker Grove. (If you look carefully during the episode where Noddy and Angel go river fishing, the sister who sits next to Angel (not the one referred to as Terraise), you'll notice a very young Adele Taylor). Adele Taylor is also Angel's sister in real-life.

Terraise was first seen as Terraise in the 1993 series in the hostage episode where she was protected and rescued by Angel.

Terraise made some appearances in the 1994 and 1995 series but didn't really become a priniciple character until 1996.

Terraise became friends with Jack although Jack later felt this friendship was threatened after Phillip and Gruff (the goat) distracted Terraise's attention.

To prevent this, Jack arranged for Gruff to be taken away by a "farmer" unrealising that he was actually from a Slaughter House! Geoff, Jack, Phillip and Terraise moved quicly and managed to save Gruff although Gruff was killed in the Racist attack on the Grove the following episode.

In 1997 Flora decided it was time for saintly behaviour and gave £20 to Phillip as a token of her new generousity as his mother had lost her job with the council. However, Flora soon learned that Phillip's mother's job had been saved and collaborated with Anna to create the "Little Treasures" prank on Jack, Terraise and Phillip, to punish them for their lies and greed.

Terraise decided that as she was getting older she needed to establish herself with a new image which attracted Ben's attention. Terraise later became a radio celeberity as the myterious "Tyneside Tina" and succeeded in bringing an end to Karen's teen advice debut.

Terraise joined Nat and Harry against the boys at the Grove who'd joined Ollie's Network O.

Terraise will be returning in Series 10 of Byker Grove.
