Episode 1: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Angel, Alfie, Anna, Flora.
Jemma heads for stardom after she is accepted on the "Screen Teen's" Project by a local TV Company. New residents, Alfie and Anna move in to Jemma's old house, but things are not right with step-mother Jean and step-sister Flora. Angel learns it's tough to make a living.

 

  Episode 2: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Gary, Alfie, Anna, Flora.
Tensions between Anna, Alfie and Flora heighten in the Turnbull household. Noddy rescues Angel from a seedy character. Jemma agrees to go on a blind date arranged by Lee and Charlie, unknowing that it is with Gary. Ed's pets make their debut appearance, and his spider almost ends up in Lee's Milkshake.

 

  Episode 3: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Charlie, Lee, Ed, Patsy, Gary
Panic breaks out at the Grove after Arran and Patsy release Ed's pets. Charlie finds romance with Lee, leaving Jemma felling betrayed. Jemma agrees to let Gary be her cameraman so she can still go ahead with Screen Teens competition.

 

  Episode 4: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Barney, Frew, Anna, Alfie, Flora
Frew and Barney's DIY music deafens the Grove. Anna and Alfie recieve wise advice from their mother from beyond the grave. Peter and Jean's wedding day dawns but it turns to disaster after Anna falls overboard in the reception boat.

 

  Episode 5: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Gary, Flora, Anna, Alfie
Jemma hears word from the Screen Teen authorities, her video has been accepted so she and camera man Gary go for a celebratory meal. Peter and Jean get an unexpected honeymoon shock when Flora turns up at the hotel.

 

  Episode 6: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Gary, Anna, Alfie, Flora
Jemma and Gary find happiness together, but it is short-lived as Jemma is electrocuted after helping an elderly neighbour. Anna's latest scheme to break up her dad's new marriage is to hold a wild part at her house during their absence, however all doesn't go according to plan.

 

  Episode 7: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Jemma, Amanda, Flora, Brigid, Gary
Jemma's funeral is well attended by her family and friends. A reception is held for her at the Grove where her Screen Teen Video is shown as a tribute to her life. Jemma, through the video gives a fond farewell to all those who enriched her life. However the sad occasion is distrupted by Flora and Brigid who get hold of Barney and Frew's DJing equipment. Amanda makes a surprising return to Newcastle, though her troubles are not far behind her.

 

  Episode 8: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Amanda, Barney, Frew, Patsy, Dale
Amanda's past catches up with her when the father of her child, Aston shows up calliing in old debts. Marie finds Barney and Frew a gig - but its at Denton Burns, and thanks to Dale's meddling, the crowd are lucky to escape alive. Patsty becomes more involved in the green issues raised by Kit and his travelling community.

 

  Episode 9: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Amanda, Noddy, Patsy, Lee, Charlie
Amanda is rescued from Aston by Noddy and Angel. Noddy starts to take an interest in Amanda, which angers Angel, especially after Noddy sends flowers to Amanda, and invites her to the fun festival. Patsy and Arran's scheme to distrupt the fun festival turns messy after Arran is mistakenly covered in manure. Lee finds Charlie a modelling job. Angel warns Amanda to keep away from Noddy.

 

  Episode 10: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Amanda, Charlie, Noddy, Leah, Patsy
A water adventure for the Grovers produces thrills, spills and tears. Amanda is finding it dificult to cope, but Noddy promises to help her whenever he can. Angel and Leah form an alliance against Amanda. A problem page strikes a familiar chord at the Grove.

 

  Episode 11: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Amanda, Noddy, Charlie, Patsy, Leah
Amanda and Noddy go on a secret date, though it seems Noddy's interest in Amanda is purely friendship. Charlie's first show ends in chaos after Patsy and her animal-right's group throw mud over the models, unrealising that they were modelling "fake furs". The mysteriously familiar problem page-letteres continue to plague the grovers. Frew and Arran are left holding the baby when Amanda needs to dash out suddenly...

 

  Episode 12: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Amanda, Barney, Frew, Dale, Leah
Amanda decides to reject Kolton's offer. Barney spins to a crashing halt in his music career after Frew's intervention. The Grove spy is revealed by Alfie and confesses all - Dale.   Episode 14: 1994 Series 6

Principle Characters: Leah, Patsy, Amanda, Dale, Anna.
Leah suddenly leaves the foster home and sets off to find her real mother. Anna and Marie become Dale's managers for his Dream Teen competition, so they can share his prize money. Dale has no option but to accept otherwise they will reveal all about the letter writing. Duncan returns to the Grove for a college reference off Geoff.   Episode 18: 1994 Series 6

  Episode 19: 1994 Series 6
