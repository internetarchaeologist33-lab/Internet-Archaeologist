Emma: Holly Wilkinson

Emma first appeared in the last four episodes of the 1997 series of Byker Grove. She was part of the Grove-Girls team against Ollie's Network O. It was later revealed that Emma was in fact Ollie's mysterious Internet Contact, Trooper John. Ollie later got his revenge on Emma by downloading a virus to the Grove's computer for which Emma was blamed. Ollie later admitted he was the culprit and apologised to Emma.

Emma will be reappearing in the 1998 series where her character will be further established.
