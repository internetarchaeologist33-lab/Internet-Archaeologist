Biography

My name is Liliya Michael B. I was born in Lugansk, Ukraine, on March 17th, in 1982. I am the only child in my family and now I live with my mother. I graduated from school in 1998. My dream since the childhood was to become a singer like Michael Jackson. This dream was born ten years ago. And now I do my best to make it come true. I always sing, I had graduated from the vocal studio and I have studied in another schools of singing. I also have the choreographic basis; I have worked in the show-ballets. In 1999-2000 I’ve been working as photo model. I had recorded 3 phonograms and took part in the music festival Golden Star in Crimea.

Now I am 19 years old and I have very various interests: the main is music, astrology, philosophy, dietology, and medicine. But I want to devote all my life to art, to singing, I dream of making shows similar to Michael Jackson’s performances. In Ukraine it is impossible to find the producer for me, because show business literally doesn’t exist in this country and it’s very hard to sing here if you have European or American style.

That’s why I am looking for opportunity to launch my career in the West world, in the USA. I learn English language. I search the producer, who could help me to make my dream a reality: to become a singer of pop-music in Michael Jackson’s style. The fact is that I want to sing not commercial songs but songs, which have inner sense and positive influence on those who listen to them. I want that my activities as an artist would be the help for this world, would bring the benefits to the people. I believe in the power of music and I wish that my music will help children and teenagers to understand and believe themselves, to comprehend the Life and muse upon the fact that we create our lives by ourselves.

I want the youngsters when they listen to my songs feel not animosity or anger but harmony and fall to thinking about their lives and their deeds. I want that my music would heal this world. I know how songs can influence human soul and mind. I feel it on myself.

I am so grateful to Michael Jackson for his contribution to the music, which has the positive influence on the young people, and I want to create exactly that kind of music. But I do not imitate Michael Jackson. I conceive my own dance sequences and shows but in MJ’s style. I want to find producers, authors and record studio in USA who would help me to make my dream a reality: to become a singer and perform the songs with great inner sense and meaning. I dance very well and I want to stage dances like Michael Jackson does. I write poems, conceive video-films and I make designs of stage outfits etc.

I hope that people who can understand and help me will find me.

The most of all I want to work with Michael Jackson as the producer and in his show.

I believe my dream is destined to come true.
