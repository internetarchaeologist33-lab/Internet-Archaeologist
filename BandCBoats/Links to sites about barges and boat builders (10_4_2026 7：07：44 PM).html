The Dutch Barge Association
http://www.barges.org/

The Dutch Barge Association is an active association for those with or wishing to buy Dutch barges either old or replica. There is a very large membership, with a newsletter issued quarterly. It has a continental cruising guide which is useful for those intending to cruise the European waterways.

The Royal Yachting Association

This is the national governing body for the UK, and covers every kind of craft, sail, motor, from dinghies and canoes up to vessels the size of Dutch Barges.

www.rya.org.uk
