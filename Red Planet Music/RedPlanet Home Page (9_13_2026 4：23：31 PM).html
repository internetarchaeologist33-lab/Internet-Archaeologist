Welcome to the Red Planet Records Home Page, virtual home of the ONLY independent record shop in Tavistock, Devon (that's Devon, England for any visitors from outside the UK or indeed from another planet).

We sell new CDs covering a wide range of musical genres at GREAT prices (all chart single albums are £12.99 or less and we have thousands of back catalogue albums from as little as £3.99). Visit our Catalogue pages for more details. And, for all of you who love vinyl for more than just making you trousers with, we have a rather interesting stock of good condition second hand 7" and 12" singles and albums that will tickle your browsing tastebuds no end!!

We're open from 9.00 - 5.30pm, Monday - Saturday so drop in to our shop when you're in town and feel free to browse. We have listening facilities and friendly, knowledgeable staff and we are very happy to locate and order for you any CDs we don't have in stock!

We're located at 20 Market St, Tavistock, Devon, PL19 0DE - just drop by our Location Map page if you need to find us ! You can contact us at any of the following addresses:
