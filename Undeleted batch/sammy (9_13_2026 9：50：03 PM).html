Sammy the cat

 

Feed Me !!

Hello !! My name is Sammy and my owner has gone overseas to try and get away from me. I'm three years old and every day I go through the sink and garbage bin in search of food. If you are eating I will follow you around like a shadow, and if you drop any crumbs I will get them before they hit the floor. I have the IQ of a stick insect, and my hobbies include eating, sleeping and puppetry. I am available for children's parties.
