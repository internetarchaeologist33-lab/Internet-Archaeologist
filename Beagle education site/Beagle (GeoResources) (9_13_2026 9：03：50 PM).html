General Statistical Links

The list below contains a variety of links to sites with general statistical information. Some of it is very detailed. Most of it is pretty up-to-date and therefore has an advantage over books with statistical data.

  • USA Census - statistics on all countries from the United States Census.

  • UK Statistics - statistics from the Government Statistical Service.

  • The IDB Page - a US Census site, full of very useful population information on every country.

  • Country Data - a useful site with a range of data on different countries.

  • Institute of Hydrology - an interesting site with a growing amount of data. You can also contact them by e-mail or snail-mail for further data.

  • Statistical Yearbook - a good site with relatively fresh data (up to 1996).

  • CIA World Factbook (1998) - the most famous (infamous)of all the data sources - recently updated - it is very detailed and is therefore useful but beware of some very long printouts if you are not selective.

  • Central and S.America - a site with information on 24 Central and South American countries.

  • OECD - statistics from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.

  • World and Country Statistics - World and Country Statistics from Infoplease.

  • Climate Statistics - Climate data for 000's of cities worldwide. An excellent source of data.

  • Global Trends - U.N. educational site with data on global trends, city profiles, country data, etc.

  • Atlapedia - information on every country in the world!

  • Geographia - information on a wide range of countries.

  • Global Statistics - an excellent site which looks at lots of different topics and ranks the data e.g. longest rivers, largest countries, highest mountains, companies with the biggest profits, highest birth rates, etc..
