From The Continent

Lanarte...
Designs in Tapestry and Counted Cross, stitch principally birds, flowers
and teddies, worked  mainly  on 28/30 count, but some designs available
on 14 count aida. The tapestries are worked on 10 count white canvas
for easy viewing. Lanarte feature the antics of Vera the Mouse by
Marjolein Bastin. You'll also love her cats and gardening arrangements.

Permin...

..Beverely...
Principally from Madeira, this range comprises Tramme Tapestry, ideal
for furnishings. For those unsure of the term, Tramme Tapestry is
when the design is worked through the fine mesh of canvas, and is
then completed in ½ cross stitch using the principal holes  of the
canvas. Mainly floral in design, the booklet offers an illustration
of the type of designs available.
A make up service for cushions etc. is available.
Details on request.
