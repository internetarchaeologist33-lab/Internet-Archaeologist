Description About Jaycraft

Jaycraft......an unusual store with a vast selection of Hand Knitting Yarns and Patterns, both English and Continental, Needlework including Needlepoint, Cross Stitch, Embroidery and Long Stitch.  Craft Kits for Decoupage, Pyrography, Candle Making, Rug Making, Lace e.t.c.

We have a large Collectible Gift Department, well known names include Discworld, Clare Craft, Enchartica, Caithness, Dartington, Myth and Magic, Matts Jonasson, Principe Porcellane, Creation de Paris, Woodsetton, Roger Lascelles, Chamberlain Clarke, Bernard Pearson Ceramics, Suzie Marsh, Colour Box, Steiff, Genesis.  Add to this a selection of traditional games including Chess, Backgamnmon, Checkers, Bridge, Labyrinth and Shut the Box, plus 3D and unusual Jig Saw Puzzles and you have a unique shopping experience all under one roof in the eastern corner of England.

Call us today , we individually serve all our customers!
