Pierre






 

 

My name is Pierre Gustafsson, and I was born and raised here in Västervik. Being born in 1968, I’m the oldest off all my siblings. I have 4 of them, one “real” and 3 half-siblings. My brother Jörgen lives in a town called Kalmar with his fiancée and works as a security guard, and my half-sister Katarina has  finished a media high school education., but now works with disabled children and teenagers.
I have another half-brother and half-sister, Conny and Maria, who I haven’t spent that much time with, since they always have lived with my father’s second wife. 

I began studying Computer Engineering after school and my military training, but I was really tired of studying, so I quit and began working in a supermarket in town for a while.
I was on my way to Australia to explore the country when I met Julia in 1990. Love made me change my mind, and a year later we moved to Stockholm and I again started studying. This time Cultural Science, and also some Computer Science, and about 5 years later I had gotten both wiser, and received my degree in Archaeology.
For the time being I’m working for a museum, both with regular archaeological tasks, and with a project where we’re making an inventory of an apartment filled with art and antiquities donated to the museum I work for. Every single item has to be registered, photographed and marked. 
A also work part-time as a croupier and dealer at a casino in town. Not as much for the money, as for the fun of it! We’re dealing with roulette and Black Jack.

Besides my genuine interest in archaeology and history, I’m also very fond of spending time in front of the computer; both surfing the Internet, and playing various games. Julia bought me a Sony Playstation for Christmas acouple of years ago, which I enjoy playing with. (“Way too much, sometimes…” –Julia’s remark). Music is very important, although most people think my taste in music is somewhat odd. What else…? I love reading books, watching good TV and films. During the summer I use to go out fishing with my father, or play beach volleyball. Our cat Cleo is also a favorite in my life…!

Want to know more, or just say hi? Mail me (see address below) or contact me via ICQ. My number is 3327201.

Julia about Pierre:

Pierre is really the sweetest, most considerate person I’ve ever met. I use to describe him as a man with a heart of gold, because he’s so incredibly kind. He always listens to me, and he never ignores me, no matter what he’s up to. He’s also really intelligent and knows almost everything! A great source of information, and a good intellectual challenge.
Pierre is my best friend, my loverboy, my life partner and now also my husband! I’m so grateful that I’ve met him, and that he’s a part of my life. I love him so much, and am so happy to be the one that gets to spend the rest of her life with this cute, handsome, adorable prince!
