Alghero, Sardinia September 14-21, 1999
 
 
 


Julia's mother wanted to "escape" from her 50th birthday, so she gathered parts of her family and fled to beautiful Sardinia outside Italy's coast for a lovely, relaxing holiday. We experienced very interesting weather while there; the day could begin with heavy rain and thunderstorms, only to brighten up into a sunny afternoon only hours later... A week is a bit short for experiencing everything, so we all long to go back sometime...
