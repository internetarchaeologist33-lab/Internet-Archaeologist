The (actual) Proposal









 
 (In case you haven't read the part prior to this one, we suggest you go ahead and do it before you continue with reading about how and when Pierre proposed to me. Click on the hedgehogs to get to that part. Thanks)

OK... back to business...

It was the day after our fight... I was still feeling a bit sad and moody (can't shake things like that off me very easily). I had an appointment at the dentist's at 4 pm that day, and when I came back home Pierre had already come home from work and came to meet me in the hallway. He told me he had a surprise for me and went down on his knees (yes, both of them; because he obviously didn't have a clue how to do -isn't that just too sweet?- *smile*) and held up a small green box tied with a ribbon and said he was sorry about everythimg and asked me if I wanted to marry him. I started to unwrap the ribbon, and open the box to see what was in it, when Pierre (still on his knees in the hallway) asked me if I wasn't going to answer him!? I had gone a bit perplexed and wanted to see what was in the box first, but then I came to my senses. 
I had hoped for a more emotional reaction, but since I was still a bit numb from the fight and all, all I did was smile, hug him and say -"of course I do!".

The whole thing was just great; OK the timing could have been better, but I think this was his way of saying he was sorry; making up for yesterday and prove that he did want to get married for real. I only wish I had been more "me" and not so blue as I was. (It took me about two more days to fully recover.) 
The really great thing is that I had been pondering to myself how Pierre was going to propose, and since we were already engaged since 3,5 years I already had an engagement ring. But I secretly thought it would be a neat thing if he bought me an engagement ring anyway, just a simple, symbolic one, so to speak, and proposed to me "the American way" since I had been reading a lot of proposal stories and really liked the way they were performed. He must have read my mind (perhaps he has some minor mind reading skills after all...? *grin*), because that's exactly what he did!!! 

I'm wearing that ring on my right hand, though, because if I wore it on my left hand, I'd have three, and that's just too much gold for one finger to bear... ;o)
 
 
 
 

 

 
So now weren't  only engaged; we were suddenly 
engaged to be married!!!
