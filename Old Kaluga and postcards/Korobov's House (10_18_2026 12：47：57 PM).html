Published by Rofe, Kaluga
Korobov's house is one of the oldest
buildings in Kaluga. There is a questionable
idea that Marina Mnishek, Lzhedimitri's I
and Lzhedimitri's II wife, lived there.
In April 1897 at Korobov's house was
founded the city historic museum.
